# Credits, licenses and media rights

Stellar Origin is an independent project and does not use the NASA logo or claim NASA endorsement.

NASA [Astronomy Picture of the Day](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/) supplies the date-specific titles, explanations and media. Each displayed record shows its own original credit/copyright text and links to the official NASA publication. APOD images are **not automatically public domain**; rights may belong to named authors. No APOD photograph or video is placed in an exported Cosmic Passport.

Astronomical computations use [Astronomy Engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy) under its open-source license. Orbital periods come from the [NASA Planetary Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/). See [methods](docs/SCIENCE_SOURCES.md) for model limitations.

The logo, hero planet, starfield, Moon graphic, orbit diagrams and passport image are original procedural vector/CSS/canvas artwork in this repository. They are illustrations, not spacecraft imagery or scientifically scaled views.

The interface uses [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/license) icons, [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/master/LICENSE) and [React Three Fiber](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-fiber/blob/master/LICENSE) with [Drei](https://github.com/pmndrs/drei/blob/master/LICENSE). Exact installed versions are recorded in `pnpm-lock.yaml`; their upstream license files govern reuse.
